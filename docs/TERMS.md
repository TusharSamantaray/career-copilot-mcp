## What Career Copilot is

A connector that gives your AI assistant memory, structure and knowledge
for careers, job searches, money basics and planning. Your assistant does
the talking. Career Copilot stores your data, applies rules and serves
reference content.

## What it is not

- **Not professional advice.** Money, tax, legal, immigration, relocation
  and health-related content is general education. Check important
  decisions with a qualified professional.
- **Not a promise.** No job, interview, offer, pay level or financial
  result is promised.
- **Not an agent that acts for you.** It drafts and suggests. You decide,
  and you send. It never applies, posts or messages anyone for you.

## Your part

- Give true information, especially for resumes and messages. Career
  Copilot won't help invent experience, numbers, titles or credentials.
- Don't use it to harass, deceive, impersonate or discriminate against
  anyone, or for anything illegal.
- Keep your sign-in, key or connector URL private. Anyone who has them
  can use your copy.
- Keep your own copy of anything important (`data_export`).

## How Career Copilot talks

It is designed to:
- keep replies short and simple: one small step and one question at a
  time, with more detail only when you ask;
- stay on your career, work, money and life-planning goals;
- be respectful and neutral, and never insult, mock or stereotype anyone;
- not comment on religion, politics, race, gender, age, health or other
  personal traits, unless you raise them for a practical reason;
- say where facts come from, say "I don't know" rather than guess, and
  label estimates;
- check drafts for insults, biased wording, promised outcomes, missing
  advice notes and numbers you never confirmed (`response_check`).

AI assistants can still make mistakes. Read drafts before you use them,
and tell us when something is wrong (ask your assistant to call
`feedback`, or open a GitHub issue).
