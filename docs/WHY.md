# Why Career Copilot exists

## The problem

Losing a job, or realising you're in the wrong one, rarely arrives as a
single problem. It arrives as twenty at once:

- the resume;
- the job boards and recruiters;
- interview prep;
- the money running down;
- where to live;
- what you even want;
- and the voice saying you're behind.

Most tools pick one of those and assume you're calm, organised and
motivated. The people who need help most are none of those things. They're
**lost, tired and overwhelmed**, and a 40-step plan makes it worse.

## The insight

Momentum doesn't come from big plans. It comes from **one small, finished
thing a day**: applying to a single role, rewriting one bullet, sending one
follow-up. You don't have to work every day of the week. You do have to
never let two days in a row go by.

AI assistants are already where people go when they're stuck. What those
assistants lack is memory, structure and a point of view: they forget your
pipeline, don't know your deadlines, and give generic advice. Career
Copilot gives any AI assistant those three things.

## The story behind it

Career Copilot started with an unexpected layoff. The founder was a
software engineer with a deadline, a long list of things to do, and no idea
where to start.

The first instinct was to build systems for everything:
- a finance dashboard;
- an application tracker pulled from the inbox;
- a story bank for interviews;
- study plans for system design and coding.

They all helped. But what actually changed the outcome was simpler: on
the days with no energy at all, still doing **one** thing. Most days
involved rest, cooking, friends and very little "work". Yet the
applications went out, the interviews got booked, and the tracker kept
moving.

The systems were the scaffolding; the daily small step was the engine. Career
Copilot packages both, so that someone who feels lost can get that same
scaffolding in one connection, plus a companion that asks each day:
*what's the one thing you can do today?*

## What we believe

1. **One small step a day beats a perfect plan.** Rest days are allowed;
   the rule is never skip twice.
2. **Your data is yours.**
   - You can export or delete it at any time.
   - Signals that improve the product are anonymous and k-anonymous.
   - It runs locally if you prefer.
3. **Bring your own AI.** It works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor,
   Muse and anything else that speaks MCP. No new app to learn.
4. **Honest over hype.**
   - Every number on a resume is one you confirmed.
   - Money, tax, visa and relocation outputs are education, not advice.
5. **Think bigger for people than they do for themselves.** If a
   different role or a different city would help someone grow, say so
   kindly and let them decide.
6. **Cheap to run, so it can be cheap to use.** No LLM bill on our side:
   the user's assistant does the talking. Hosting runs from $0 to $49 a
   month.

## Who it's for

People in transition: laid off, early in a career, stuck in the wrong
role, changing fields, or returning to work. Any field, any country.
